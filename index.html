1 · Predict

How high can you jump? Guess in centimetres.

40cm (your guess)

2 · Measure

Hold the phone tight against your chest. Press the button, wait for "Jump!", jump straight up and land on the same spot.

The line is what the phone feels. It drops to almost zero while you are in the air: you and the phone are weightless. The grey band is your airtime.

Your jump

You go up for half the airtime and come down for the other half. Falling for a time t covers ½·g·t², so with half the airtime: h = g·t²/8.
